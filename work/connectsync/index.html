ConnectSyncP2P via Google Drive

A peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted folder synchronization tool powered by Google Drive. No servers, no accounts needed—just your drive and a sync code. Everything is AES-256 encrypted before it leaves your machine.

what it is

Share folders instantly, securely.

Most sync tools require you to create an account, pay for a subscription, or trust a third-party server. ConnectSync uses your existing Google Drive as a dumb, encrypted transport layer. Your files are encrypted with AES-256 locally, uploaded, and can only be decrypted by someone with your specific sync code.

features

Private by design.

E2E ENCRYPTED

Zero-Knowledge

Every file is encrypted on your machine using AES-GCM-256 before leaving. Even Google cannot read your files.

Peer-to-Peer Magic

Just share the generated sync code. No email invites, no permission setups. The code grants access and decrypts.

Native UI

Built with Slint and Rust. It's incredibly fast, uses minimal memory, and looks native on both Windows and Linux.
