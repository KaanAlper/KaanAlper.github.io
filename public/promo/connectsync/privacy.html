Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the ConnectSync desktop application and describes how your information is handled.

1. Data We Collect

ConnectSync does not collect, transmit, or store any personal telemetry, usage data, or analytics on external servers. Everything happens directly between your device and your Google Drive account.

2. Google Drive Data

ConnectSync requests access to your Google Drive to store and synchronize your files. When you authorize the app:

  • We only use the drive.file scope, which means ConnectSync can only see, edit, and create files that it has created or that you specifically open with it. It cannot see your entire Google Drive.
  • Your files are encrypted locally on your machine using AES-GCM-256 before being uploaded to Google Drive.
  • The encryption keys are generated locally and never sent to any central server. They are only shared when you share your "Sync Code" with another peer.

3. Local Storage

Your authentication tokens are stored securely in your operating system's native credential manager (Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service, or macOS Keychain). Sync configurations are stored locally on your disk.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone because we do not have it. The only way data is shared is if you intentionally give someone else your Sync Code.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can check the GitHub repository or contact the developer.
